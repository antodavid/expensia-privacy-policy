Expensia Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 October 2026. Applies to the Expensia Android app (package com.fact.numbers.numbersfact), version 2.0.0 and later. Expensia replaces the app previously published as Numbers Fact; this policy replaces the Numbers Fact privacy policy of 13 July 2022.

In short: Your personal expenses, events, planned spends and subscriptions stay on your phone. To make shared splits work, the app keeps a small profile on our server (a random user ID, your display name and, only if you add it, your phone number) plus the splits you take part in. The app shows ads through Google AdMob, which collects some device data as described below. We never read your SMS, email, notifications or contact list, and we never connect to your bank.

1. Who we are

Expensia is developed and published by the developer listed on the app's Google Play page ("we", "us"). Contact: imdevaraj444@gmail.com.

2. Data that stays on your phone

These are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. We do not receive them:

  • Expenses: amounts, categories, notes, dates and receipts you attach
  • Events (trips, projects, budgets) and the names of people you split with inside an event
  • Planned spends you add on the calendar, and their reminders
  • Subscriptions you track and their renewal reminders
  • Your gender, if you choose to set it
  • Your questions to "Ask AI" and the AI insights. They are calculated on your device; nothing is sent to us or to any AI service. To keep the feature fair, Ask AI answers up to 5 questions a day, plus 2 more if you choose to watch a short rewarded ad; the count is kept on your device.

3. Data stored on our server

Your profile. The first time the app is online, it creates a profile for you on our server so that shared splits work and so that your data can move to a full account if we add one later. It contains:

  • A random user ID created by the server (not linked to your name, email or device identifiers)
  • Your display name: the name or nickname you enter, or a generated name such as user_123456789 if you skip it
  • Your phone number, only if you choose to add it in your profile. It is optional, it is never shown to other users, and it is kept so that you can be identified by your number if accounts are added in the future. You can remove it at any time by clearing the field.

Shared splits. When you start or join a split, we store its title, currency and category, the items people add (description, amount, who it is split between), who has paid whom, and a history of changes. Everyone in that split, including people who join from a web browser, can see the members' display names and the items. Only the person who added an item can change it.

Where and how. This data is stored on Amazon Web Services (AWS) in Mumbai, India (region ap-south-1) and is only ever sent over encrypted HTTPS. If you use the app outside India, your profile and split data are transferred to and processed in India. AWS acts as our service provider and does not use the data for its own purposes. Changes you make offline are kept on your phone and sent when you are back online.

4. Inviting people

To invite someone to a split you can share a link through WhatsApp, SMS or any other app, choose a contact, or type a number. When you pick a contact, Android's own contact picker gives the app only the one phone number you selected, to open WhatsApp or SMS with the invite. Expensia does not request permission to read your contacts, does not read your contact list, and does not upload the number you picked.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Expensia is free and supported by ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect and process:

  • Advertising ID and other device identifiers: ad delivery, frequency capping, measurement and fraud prevention
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address: ad relevance and legal compliance
  • Interactions with ads, and crash and performance diagnostics of the ads SDK: analytics and fraud prevention

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's certified consent tool before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google settings. Learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Rewarded videos play only when you ask for one: "Take a day off ads" hides ads for 24 hours, and in Ask AI a rewarded video unlocks 2 more questions for the day. People who use Ask AI regularly may also see an ad when they close the chat.

6. Notifications

Subscription renewal reminders and planned-spend reminders are created on your device from data on your device. Split updates (for example "Ravi added Taxi") come from the app checking our server for changes to splits you are in, using your random user ID. No push-notification service is used.

7. Permissions

  • Internet / network state: shared splits and ads
  • Notifications: reminders and split updates. Asked for only when you turn on a reminder or plan a spend.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob as described above

Expensia does not request access to SMS, call logs, contacts, location (GPS), camera or microphone.

8. Sharing

We do not sell your data and do not share it with third parties for their own purposes. Data leaves your phone only as described above: our server (for profiles and splits), Google AdMob (for ads), and any app you choose when you export a report (CSV), export your data (JSON) or share a split summary or invite. We may disclose data if required by law.

9. Retention and deletion

  • Data on your phone stays until you delete it (Settings → Delete my data) or uninstall the app. We hold no copy and cannot recover it; use Settings → Export my data to keep one.
  • Your profile and split data are kept while you use the app. To delete everything, open Settings → Delete my data in the app: it deletes your profile (name and phone number) from our server, replaces your name with "Former member" in the splits you were in, and erases all data on your phone. The items you added to shared splits stay so other people's balances still add up. You can also ask us to do it: email imdevaraj444@gmail.com with the subject "Expensia account deletion" and include the display name shown at the top of the app's side menu (and your phone number if you added one). We delete it within 30 days.

10. Security

Data on your phone is kept in Android's app-private storage, and your server credentials are stored encrypted. The app opts out of Android device and cloud backup, so data you delete is not restored later; use Settings → Export my data to keep your own copy. All network traffic uses HTTPS, and the app blocks unencrypted connections. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we use commercially reasonable measures to protect your data.

11. Children

Expensia is intended for adults (18 and over) and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children; if you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, the GDPR, the UK GDPR or the CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. You can correct your name and phone number in the app at any time, and delete all your data in Settings → Delete my data. For anything else, email us at imdevaraj444@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days.

13. Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page, update the effective date, and show a notice in the app for significant changes.

14. Contact
